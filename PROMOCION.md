## BetaList (inglés)

**Name:** Alinvest

**Tagline:** Track your funds and ETFs and plan your monthly DCA

**Description:**
> Alinvest is a free web app for people who invest a little every month in index funds and ETFs. Enter how many units you hold and your average price: every day it updates the value of your portfolio with the closing price and shows your gain, like your broker does. Type this month's contribution and it tells you how much goes to each fund, according to the bands you set. It also forecasts when you will reach your goal. No bank connection, no ads, data stored in the EU, everything exportable as CSV.

## Product Hunt (inglés)

**Tagline:** Your portfolio, organised: auto-valued funds and monthly DCA

**Description:**
> A free app to keep track of your index funds, ETFs and stocks. Enter your units once and it updates your portfolio every day, tells you how to split this month's contribution between your funds, and shows when you'll reach your goal. No bank connection, no ads.

**First comment (maker):**
> Hi everyone! I built Alinvest for myself. I invest every month in a handful of index funds and ETFs, and every month I opened a spreadsheet to work out how much to put in each one so my portfolio stayed within its bands. It worked, but it was fiddly and I always forgot to update the values.
>
> So Alinvest does three things: it keeps the value of each fund up to date on its own (units × closing price), it splits your monthly contribution according to your bands, and it tells you when you'll reach your goal at your current pace.
>
> It doesn't connect to your bank or move money — it only keeps the accounts. Data stays in the EU and you can export everything as CSV. It's a free beta, and I'd love to hear what you'd improve.

**Topics:** Personal Finance, Investing, Fintech, Productivity

## Hacker News, Show HN (inglés)

**Title:** Show HN: Alinvest – track index funds/ETFs and split your monthly DCA by bands

**Text:**
> I invest monthly in a few index funds and used a spreadsheet to decide how much to put in each so every block stays within its band. Alinvest is that spreadsheet turned into a small web app: daily valuation from units × closing price (with currency conversion for GBp/USD listings), a DCA split that respects min/max bands, minimum amounts and multiples, and a goal forecast.
>
> It's a Python/Starlette backend with SQLite and a vanilla JS front end, hosted in the EU. No bank connections and no tracking. Free beta; feedback on the allocation logic is especially welcome.
