# Alinvest

**Keep track of your funds, ETFs and stocks, and plan your monthly contribution in a minute.**

Alinvest is a free web app for people who invest a little every month in index funds and ETFs. Enter what you hold once: it shows what your portfolio is worth, what you have gained, and how to split this month's contribution between your funds.

## What it does

- **Automatic value.** Enter how many units you hold of each fund, ETF or stock and your average purchase price. Every day Alinvest looks up the closing price and updates your portfolio on its own. You see your gain since you bought, just like in your broker.
- **Monthly DCA.** Type what you are going to invest this month and it tells you how much goes to each fund, according to the bands you set for each block (equity, bonds, emerging markets…).
- **Bands and weights.** See how much each block weighs in your portfolio and whether it is inside its band.
- **Your goal.** When you will reach your target at your current pace, and how much extra you would need to get there sooner.
- **Fund page.** For each fund: what you have contributed, what it has earned, its weight, its market performance (1, 3 and 5 years) and its month-by-month history.
- **Buy by units.** Enter the units and the date of a purchase: the app finds that day's closing price and shows you the amount before saving anything. It handles listings in other currencies (USD, GBP, GBp) and converts them.
- **Emergency fund and crypto.** Where you keep your cushion and how much it will earn by year end; Bitcoin, Ethereum and Cardano valued with the live price.
- **On your phone and on your computer.** Install it on your phone like an app. On a computer it uses the whole screen. Dark mode, English and Spanish.

## Your data is yours

- It **does not connect to your bank** and never moves your money: it only keeps the accounts.
- **No ads and no tracking.**
- Data is stored on servers **in the European Union**, with daily backups.
- **Download everything as CSV** or delete your account whenever you want. You can also import your values and contributions from a CSV.

Alinvest is a tool to get organised, not financial advice. The decisions are yours.

## Getting started

1. Go to [alinvest.app](https://alinvest.app) and ask for an invitation (the beta opens places gradually), or use an invitation link if someone sent you one.
2. Choose a starting template (for example, a classic 60/40) or start from scratch.
3. Add your funds by name or ISIN, enter their units, and you are done.
